# AI personal assistants — online services

Cloud-based AI assistants for productivity, notes, research, presentations,
and everyday tasks. No local GPU required.

> See also: [1_local_tools.md](1_local_tools.md) §5 for local/offline assistants,
> [3_ai_creative_tools.md](3_ai_creative_tools.md) for image/video/audio.

Free tiers marked **[free]**; partially-free marked **[freemium]**.

---

## 1. General personal AI assistants

* [Pi](https://pi.ai/) **[free]** — conversational AI focused on emotional intelligence and long-term memory; voice mode
* [Claude Projects](https://claude.ai) **[freemium]** — persistent context, custom instructions per project; best for ongoing work
* [ChatGPT (with Memory)](https://chatgpt.com) **[freemium]** — persistent memory, custom GPTs, browsing, code interpreter
* [Gemini Advanced](https://gemini.google.com) **[freemium]** — integrates with Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Drive)
* [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com) **[free]** — integrated into Windows, Edge, Office 365
* [Poe](https://poe.com/) **[freemium]** — one interface to Claude, GPT-4, Llama, Mistral, etc.; free daily quota

---

## 2. AI search & research

* [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) **[freemium]** — AI search with cited sources; Spaces for team research
* [Phind](https://www.phind.com/) **[free]** — developer-focused AI search
* [Exa](https://exa.ai/) **[freemium]** — semantic web search API + chat; great for research pipelines
* [You.com](https://you.com/) **[freemium]** — AI search with research and coding modes

---

## 3. AI notes & knowledge base

* [Notion AI](https://www.notion.ai/) **[freemium]** — AI built into Notion: summarize, draft, Q&A over your notes
* [Mem.ai](https://mem.ai/) **[freemium]** — AI-powered notes with automatic connections between related content
* [Obsidian + local LLM plugin](https://obsidian.md/) **[free/OSS]** — Markdown notes; community plugins for Ollama/Claude integration

---

## 4. AI presentations & docs

* [Gamma](https://gamma.app/) **[freemium]** — AI-generated presentations, docs, and websites; 400 free credits at signup
* [Tome](https://tome.app/) **[freemium]** — AI narrative presentations and one-pagers
* [Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai/) **[freemium]** — smart slide design with AI layout suggestions
* [Google Slides + Gemini](https://workspace.google.com/) **[freemium]** — Gemini in Slides can generate text and images inline

---

## 5. AI coding assistants (web / cloud-based)

* [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) **[freemium]** — free tier in VS Code: 2k completions + 50 chat messages/mo
* [Replit AI](https://replit.com/) **[freemium]** — in-browser coding + AI assistant + deployments
* [StackBlitz](https://stackblitz.com/) **[freemium]** — in-browser dev environment with AI
* [v0 by Vercel](https://v0.dev/) **[freemium]** — generate React UI components from a text prompt

---

## 6. AI scheduling & productivity

* [Reclaim.ai](https://reclaim.ai/) **[freemium]** — AI schedules focus time and meetings in Google Calendar
* [Motion](https://www.usemotion.com/) **[paid]** — AI project planner + calendar optimizer
* [Superhuman](https://superhuman.com/) **[paid]** — AI-first email client (fast keyboard-driven UX)
* [Shortwave](https://www.shortwave.com/) **[freemium]** — AI email with summaries and smart compose (Gmail)
